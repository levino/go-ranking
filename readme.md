# go-liga

**Run a real Go league at your school — on one tablet, with real stones,
and ratings that actually mean something.**

A tablet sits on the table, signed in to the teacher's account. When two
kids want to play, they tap their way to a fair handicap, play the game
with real stones, then record who won — and watch their ranks move on the
spot. go-liga keeps a proper, tournament-grade rating for every child and
turns every game into an instant update. No paper, no data-entry evening,
no logins for the kids.

## Why it's cool

🏆 **Real ratings, not made-up numbers.** Every player carries a proper
strength rating on the kyu/dan scale — the same OGS Glicko-2 system that
rates games on online-go.com, the world's biggest Go server. Beat someone
stronger and your rating jumps; the maths is honest and the leaderboard is
real.

⚖️ **Fair games, every time.** go-liga reads the gap between two players and
tells you the handicap and komi that make the game a genuine contest — so a
beginner and a club veteran can sit down and both have a chance. No more
lopsided blowouts.

📱 **One tablet, the whole club.** The teacher signs in once and the tablet
stays on the table as a self-serve kiosk. Kids pick themselves, pick their
opponent, get the handicap, play, and tap in the result — no logins, no
accounts, no grown-up needed for every game.

📈 **A living leaderboard.** The ranking updates the instant a game is
recorded. Players watch themselves climb or slip after every result —
rivalries form, and everyone can see exactly where they stand.

🤖 **Talk to your league.** go-liga speaks MCP, so you can plug it straight
into Claude and friends. Ask "who's top of the league?", "record that Mia
beat Tom", or "what handicap should these two play?" — all in plain
language, no forms required.

🔢 **Multiple groups, one place.** Run separate leagues for different
classes, clubs, or age groups side by side, each with its own players and
its own ranking.

🌍 **English & German.** The whole interface — and the handbook — speaks
both languages. Each account picks its own; switch any time from a tap in
the header or the settings page.

## How it works

1. A tablet sits on the table, signed in to the teacher's account.
2. Two kids want to play: they pick themselves and their opponent, and
   go-liga shows the fair handicap and komi.
3. They play the game with real stones.
4. They tap in who won — and immediately see their ratings and ranks move.

That's the whole loop. No paper, no data-entry evening, no logins for the
kids. (Admins can still manage players and groups, or record games in bulk,
by asking Claude over MCP.)
